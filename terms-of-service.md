# Spiking Facts Studio: Terms of Service

*Last updated: November 16, 2026*

Spiking Facts Studio ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of the Spiking Facts channels. It creates short vertical clips from long-form videos and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

## Use

- The app is for personal use by its owner, on accounts the owner controls.
- Every publication is reviewed and confirmed by the owner before it is posted.
- Content is published in line with each platform's terms and community guidelines, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

## Content and credit

- Clips credit the original creators in their captions.
- The owner is responsible for having the right to publish each clip, for example through creator clipping programs or fair-use commentary, and will remove any clip on a valid request from a rights holder.

The app is provided "as is", without warranty. It may change or stop at any time.
