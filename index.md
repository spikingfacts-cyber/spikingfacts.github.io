# Spiking Facts

The moments everyone's talking about, clipped daily: streamers, podcasts and what's trending right now.

Spiking Facts Studio is the personal desktop tool behind the channel. It turns long videos into short vertical clips and, after review, posts them to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
