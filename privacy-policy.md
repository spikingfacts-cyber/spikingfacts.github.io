# Spiking Facts Studio: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 16, 2026*

Spiking Facts Studio ("the app") is a personal desktop tool run by the owner of the Spiking Facts channels. It runs only on the owner's own computer. It turns long-form videos into short vertical clips and uploads those clips to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

## What the app accesses

When you connect a TikTok account, the app asks TikTok for permission to:
- read your basic profile and public statistics: display name, follower count, likes and video count (`user.info.basic`, `user.info.stats`);
- list your videos and their view, like and comment counts (`video.list`);
- upload videos to your account or your drafts (`video.upload`, `video.publish`).

## How data is used

- The data is used only to upload the clips you choose and to show your own statistics in the app.
- Access tokens are stored only on the computer running the app, in a file readable only by its user.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- The app has no servers. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the TikTok, YouTube and Instagram APIs, when you publish or refresh your statistics.

## Deleting your data

Use the "Disconnect" button in the app to delete the stored token from the computer. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services.
